# Markings, Decals, and Text Patterns

Load this pack for logos, labels, symbols, stripes, panel markings, or
identity-critical text.

Route silhouette-neutral markings to decals or material layers. Use geometry
only when embossing, engraving, or raised paint changes shadows or silhouette.
Preserve placement, orientation, scale, contrast, and host relationship inside
the viewing contract.

Executable corrections route through `place-decal` or
`retune-marking-contrast`. Reject mirrored text, floating decals, arbitrary
symbols, incorrect perspective, and markings used to distract from wrong form.
