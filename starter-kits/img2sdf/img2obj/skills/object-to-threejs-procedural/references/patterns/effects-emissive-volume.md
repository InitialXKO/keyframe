# Emissive and Volume Effect Patterns

Load this pack for glow, energy cores, aura, smoke, fog, or another visible
effect whose identity depends on emitted light or bounded volume.

Use geometry for silhouette-bearing housings and material/effect response for
the luminous or volumetric layer. Preserve the reference bloom footprint,
interior visibility, color hierarchy, and occlusion. Effects may enhance a
correct form but may never conceal a missing signature component.

Executable corrections route through `retune-emission` or
`retune-volume-density`. Reject clipped bloom, flat neon paint, opaque fog,
unbounded particles, or brightness used to erase internal structure.
